## Workflow hypotheses to evaluate

- Case resolution: can the workflow distinguish customer reports, payment
  observations and policy conditions before producing a safe draft?
- Escalation: does a handoff include enough redacted evidence and missing details
  for the next team to act without inventing root cause or severity?
- Options comparison: does the plan enforce mandatory requirements before
  weighting preferences and make unknown evidence visible?
- Research brief: are claims supported by resolvable sources, with observational
  and causal evidence kept separate?
- Launch content: do fact and brand reviews reject unsupported claims and carry
  their edits into the revised article?
- Repurposing: do every channel's drafts preserve source meaning and caveats
  while satisfying channel constraints?

Use realistic fixtures, human assessment and real runs to measure these.
Deterministic fictional samples demonstrate interactions and expected shape.
